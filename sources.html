Sources

Every figure on this site is listed here with where it came from, how sure we are, and when we last checked. Some figures are estimates, projections or several years old; the page where each appears says so. Findings by advocacy groups are attributed to them. Figures that change, such as export quotas and AI energy use, are re-checked every three months.
