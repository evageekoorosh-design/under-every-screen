Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it on the page where it appears, and we record the change here with the date, what was wrong, and what it now says.

Report an error

Email evageekoorosh@gmail.com. Please say which page and figure you mean, and send a source if you have one. We aim to reply within a week.

How we handle changes

Figures that move, such as export quotas, phone shipments and data center forecasts, are re-checked every three months. Every figure on the sources page shows when it was last checked.
